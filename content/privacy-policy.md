# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 8, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Pipery ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our websites including but not limited to:
- https://pipery.dev
- https://start.pipery.dev

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our websites, we automatically collect certain information about your device and your use of the site, including:

- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type and version, operating system
- **Usage Information**: Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, search queries, referring URL
- **Location Information**: General location based on IP address
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies**: We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and analyze site usage

### 2.2 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc. ("Google"). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to analyze how users use our websites. The information generated about your use of the website (including your IP address) is transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States.

Google may also transfer this information to third parties where required to do so by law, or where such third parties process the information on Google's behalf.

**Data Processed by Google Analytics:**
- IP address
- Device and browser information
- Pages visited
- Time on page
- Referrer information
- Interaction data

### 2.3 Information You Provide Voluntarily

If you contact us, subscribe to our newsletter, or otherwise voluntarily provide information, we may collect:
- Name and contact information
- Email address
- Messages and feedback
- Project or workflow information you submit

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

1. **Website Analytics**: To understand how users interact with our websites and improve user experience
2. **Personalization**: To tailor content and features to your interests
3. **Communication**: To respond to your inquiries and send you requested information
4. **Performance Monitoring**: To monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities
5. **Security**: To detect, prevent, and address fraud and security issues
6. **Compliance**: To comply with legal obligations

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your data based on:

- **Legitimate Interests**: Analytics and performance monitoring
- **Consent**: For non-essential cookies and tracking technologies
- **Legal Obligation**: To comply with applicable laws

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. However, we may share your information with:

- **Google Analytics**: As described above
- **Service Providers**: Platforms and tools we use to operate our websites
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, regulation, or legal process

## 6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

### 6.1 Types of Cookies We Use

- **Essential Cookies**: Necessary for website functionality
- **Analytics Cookies**: To understand how you use our websites (Google Analytics)
- **Preference Cookies**: To remember your settings and preferences

### 6.2 Consent Management

We use tarteaucitron.js, a consent management solution, to manage your cookie preferences. You can:
- Accept all cookies
- Reject all non-essential cookies
- Customize your preferences
- Withdraw your consent at any time

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 For EEA, UK, Switzerland Residents

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request access to your personal data
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data (right to be forgotten)
- **Restriction**: Request restriction of processing
- **Portability**: Request a copy of your data in a portable format
- **Withdrawal**: Withdraw consent at any time
- **Objection**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@pipery.dev

## 8. Data Retention
